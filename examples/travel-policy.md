# Travel expense policy — demonstration version

Hotel accommodation is limited to EUR 100 per night. Taxi expenses are limited
to EUR 50 per expense. Meals are limited to EUR 35 per attendee.
Itemized receipts are required for hotel, taxi, and meal expenses.
Expenses must fall within the submitted trip dates and have a business purpose.
Repeated receipts require a manager to verify that there is no duplicate claim.
Corporate card and travel-booking records must be checked manually until those
systems are integrated. Missing receipts or uncertain extraction require review.
A manager makes the final decision; approval does not execute reimbursement.

Structured limits in the admin form must match this text:
- hotel | EUR | 100 | PER_NIGHT | receipt required
- taxi | EUR | 50 | PER_EXPENSE | receipt required
- meal | EUR | 35 | PER_ATTENDEE | receipt required
